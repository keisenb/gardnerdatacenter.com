Say no to a data center in Gardner.

460 acres. Industrial zoning. No plan.

Dec 15 · 7:00 p.m. Planning Commission, Gardner City Hall.

Plan to attend Dec 15 Sign in person

Why “no plan” matters

With straight M-1, the uses M-1 allows can come later with only building-by-building site plan review, after the zoning is already decided.

A planned district (MP-1) requires a preliminary development plan with the rezoning, and the final plan has to match it. That is when buffers, traffic, drainage, and conditions get set in public.

460 acres of straight M-1 with no plan is a blank check.

What we’re asking for

We are not against development. We are asking the City to:

  1. Plan this area before rezoning it.
  2. Say clearly where data centers belong in Gardner’s code.
  3. If anything is approved, require planned zoning (MP-1) with a real development plan, buffers, and conditions — not straight M-1.

Questions to ask the City

  • The signed application
  • The staff report
  • Maps for every parcel included
  • Any proposed conditions
  • How the City would classify a data center use under M-1
  • Utility capacity and who pays for upgrades
  • Any submitted site, traffic, and drainage studies
  • What notice neighbors will get before anything is built

Two different petitions

1

Gardner residents sign in person.

This puts a question on the ballot: whether to prohibit battery storage and hyperscale data centers in Gardner for 10 years. Only city residents who are registered voters can vote. Signing is in person.
