What is on the calendar.

  1. Tue, Dec 15, 2026 · 7:00 p.m. · Gardner City Hall

    Gardner Planning Commission public hearing, Case 26-303-02

    Public comment is part of the record. Plan to attend; if you want to speak, arrive early. The Commission has 7 seats. A recommendation takes at least 4 yes votes — a majority of all the seats, not of whoever shows up. The City lists 6 commissioners right now, so one seat may be open. It is still 4. The vote is a recommendation. The City Council makes the final call.

    Per the public hearing notice published Nov 17, 2026. Confirm the agenda on the City website.

  2. TBD / confirm with the City Clerk

    Landowner protest petition

    This is separate from the in-person petition Gardner residents sign. Owners of land within 1,000 feet of the site can sign. They do not have to live in Gardner. If owners of at least 20% of the land being rezoned, or 20% of the notice area around it, sign within 14 days after the Dec 15 hearing, the rezoning needs three-quarters of the Governing Body. Without that petition, approval normally takes a simple majority. To reject the Planning Commission’s recommendation without a petition, the Council needs two-thirds.

    The Governing Body is Mayor Winters plus 5 council members. If the mayor counts, three-quarters of 6 is 5 votes. If it is only the 5 council members, it is 4 of 5. Either way, 2 no votes can stop it. Confirm with the City Clerk whether the mayor counts. These figures are from an unofficial reading of the code.
