Meetings & milestones.

Beale Infrastructure has withdrawn from Gardner. The entries below are a historical schedule from when the rezoning was live. City and school meetings may still occur on other topics — always use the official published agenda. Calendar buttons remain for dates that still make sense to track.

Schedule

Schedule as we know it.

  1. April 2026

    Project publicly announced

    The data center project is publicly announced. Residents and the broader community begin to learn the details of what is proposed.

    Read the proposal
  2. May 2026

    Developer withdrew from Gardner project

    After the City removed economic incentives, Beale Infrastructure ended its pursuit of the Gardner site. Developer-hosted information sessions that had been announced did not go forward.
